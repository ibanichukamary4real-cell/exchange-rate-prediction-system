Method: multilayer perceptron (4 inputs, tan-sigmoid hidden layer, 4 linear outputs) trained with the Levenberg–Marquardt algorithm. This year's rates are the inputs and next year's rates are the targets. Data are scaled to −1…1 and divided at random into 60% training, 20% validation and 20% test, with early stopping after 6 validation failures. A linear trend (first-degree polynomial) is shown for comparison. B.Sc. Computer Science project by Lobarine Emmanuel Kinane. Predictions are indications for planning, not financial advice.
